# Swish-Expansion-Strategy-Location-Intelligence-
🚀 What makes a market attractive for expansion — high demand, the right customer base, or better economics?

I explored this question through a self-initiated Delhi NCR Expansion Analysis for Swish, evaluating 50 micro-markets to identify promising locations for its hyperlocal food-delivery model.
⚡ Swish is a food-tech startup built around the 10-minute food delivery model. Its neighbourhood-kitchen approach makes location selection critical — kitchens need to be positioned close to concentrated and relevant demand.

📊 The Dashboard
I built an interactive Power BI dashboard that converts the market research into a location-selection and decision-support tool.
The dashboard explores KPIs across six core dimensions:
🎯 Demand Potential
👥 Target Customer Fit
🏢 Office Demand
🍽️ Food Delivery Ecosystem
💰 Cost Attractiveness
📍 Connectivity
These factors were combined into a weighted Expansion Score, allowing the 50 markets to be ranked and classified into Priority Expansion, Strong Candidate, Investigate, and Lower Priority segments.
The dashboard also includes market rankings, city-level analysis, residential price proxies, priority segmentation, and an interactive Market Deep Dive to understand why an individual location scores the way it does.
🔍 Alongside Power BI, I built a custom Location Comparison Tool, allowing any two micro-markets to be selected and compared side-by-side across all six factors through an interactive hexagonal profile.
The idea was to go beyond simply asking “Where is demand highest?” and instead explore “Where do demand, customer fit, ecosystem strength, accessibility and economics come together?”
🛠️ Tools: Power BI | Excel | DAX | HTML/CSS/JavaScript | Market Research
This is an independent analytical project based on publicly available information and analyst-assigned proxy scores. No internal Swish data was used.
